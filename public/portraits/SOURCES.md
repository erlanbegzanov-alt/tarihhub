# Portrait image sources

51 of the 51 TarihHub figures now have an image. All portraits were regenerated by the
project team as AI-restyled illustrations (2026-08-01/02) to give every figure a single,
consistent visual style, replacing the earlier mix of real photos, monument photos and
postage-stamp scans. **These do not have a tracked original source/license** — they were
generated by the user's team via Gemini, not sourced or verified by me. Listed here for
transparency, not as a rights clearance.

For figures with a real surviving photograph or life portrait (the `portrait` kind
below), the AI restyle was based on that real reference image, so the likeness is
grounded in an actual historical photo. For figures with no surviving contemporary
depiction (the `monument` kind — mostly khans, biys and batyrs from before photography),
the AI image is a stylistic illustration only, not a claim about how the person actually
looked — the app labels these accordingly so a generated image is never presented as a
real likeness.

Format: `<personId> — <kind>`
